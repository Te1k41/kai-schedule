# Progress Tracking Template

## Usage Guide
- Fill in the **Daily Workout Log** right after each Mon/Wed/Fri session — designed to take under 2 minutes (checkboxes + single-line entries, no free text required).
- Fill in the **Daily Condition Check** every night before bed (30 seconds — 5 numbers + 1 checkbox).
- Fill in **Body Composition** every Sunday (weight — judge the 3-week trend, never a single reading) and monthly (waist).
- Run the **Periodic Evaluation** at the end of Weeks 2, 6, 9, 10, and 12 — these are the architect's actual phase boundaries (not generic 4-week marks), so each evaluation lines up with a real transition: Adaptation→Accumulation, Accumulation→Intensification, Intensification→Deload, Deload→Re-accumulation, Re-accumulation→Week 13 restart.
- No external load anywhere in this program — every log field tracks **rounds, reps/hold-time, and difficulty rung** instead of weight.

### Measurement Guide
- **Weight**: Sunday morning, immediately upon waking, after bathroom, fasted. Judge the **3-week rolling trend**, never a single Sunday reading — a single week of noise (water, sodium, sleep) is not a result.
- **Waist**: Monthly, at navel level, relaxed (not sucked in), same time of day as weigh-in. Better recomp signal than scale weight alone since the goal is fat loss + muscle retention, not pure weight loss.
- **Trend read**: if the 3-week trend shows less than ~0.3%/week loss, that's the signal to tighten intake ~5% (per nutrition plan) — not a reason to panic on any single week.
- **Alternative to calipers**: not needed here — waist tape + 3-week weight trend is the recomp signal this plan uses; no body-fat % tracking required.
