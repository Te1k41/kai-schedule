# Exercise Guide Document

Covers all 7 movement patterns used in Kai's 12-week bodyweight program (see `01_program_design.md` / `02_weekly_schedule.md`). Each entry is a **progression ladder** — the Alternative Exercises table IS the ladder (easy → hard = the rungs Kai advances through via double progression). Bodyweight-only, no jumping/plyometrics anywhere (protects joints at BMI ~30 and the fragile-home floor/neighbors).

## Warm-Up Routine (5–10 min)
| # | Movement | Time/Reps | Purpose |
|---|----------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | March in place (no jumping) | 2 min | Raise heart rate, warm whole body — jump rope/jogging skipped: floor impact rule |
| 2 | Hip circles + leg swings (front/back, side/side) | 10 reps each direction | Warm hip joint before squat/hinge work |
| 3 | Cat-cow spinal roll | 8 reps | Mobilize lower back before Superman/dead bug |
| 4 | Arm circles + cross-body shoulder swings | 10 reps each direction | Warm shoulder before push-up/pull work |
| 5 | Bodyweight half-squat (shallow, no hold) | 10 reps | Groove squat pattern, warm knees |
| 6 | Glute bridge (slow, no hold) | 10 reps | Activate glutes before Hinge exercises |

## Exercise 01: Push-up Progression (starts: Wall Push-up)
> Difficulty: ★☆☆ | Target: Pectoralis major | Equipment: None (wall / stable counter for early rungs)
### Target Muscles
- **Prime Mover**: Pectoralis major (chest muscle)
- **Synergist**: Triceps brachii (back of upper arm), Anterior deltoid (front shoulder)
- **Stabilizer**: Serratus anterior (rib-cage stabilizer), Rectus abdominis / obliques (core, keeps hips from sagging)
### How to Perform
1. **Starting Position**: Face a wall (or stable counter/table for incline rung), hands slightly wider than shoulder-width, arms extended, body in one straight line from head to heels.
2. **Execution (Concentric)**: Press through palms to straighten the elbows, pushing the surface away — exhale.
3. **Peak Contraction**: Brief pause with arms extended, shoulder blades slightly apart (not pinched), 1 second.
4. **Return (Eccentric)**: Bend elbows at roughly 45° from the torso (not flared to 90°) and lower under control until chest nears the surface — inhale.
5. **Repetition**: Keep hips and shoulders level throughout — no sagging or piking. Reset core brace each rep.
### Breathing
- On the way up (press away): exhale
- On the way down (lower in): inhale
### Common Mistakes
| Mistake | Risk | Correction Cue |
|---------|------|----------------|
| Elbows flared to 90° from body | Shoulder impingement | "Elbows point back at ~45°, not out to the sides" |
| Neck craning forward to look up | Cervical strain | "Eyes toward the floor/surface, chin tucked slightly" |
| Hips sagging or piking up | Lower-back strain, wastes the rep | "Straight line from head to heel — brace like a plank" |
| Hands too narrow or too wide | Uneven shoulder loading | "Hands roughly shoulder-width, directly under shoulders at the bottom" |

⚠️ This pattern is the most likely to expose shoulder discomfort in a true beginner. If any shoulder pain (not muscle fatigue) is felt, drop back one rung immediately — do not push through.
### Alternative Exercises
| Difficulty | Exercise | Equipment | Notes |
|------------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Easy | Wall push-up | Wall | Start here (Week 1). Steepest angle = least load. |
| Moderate | Incline push-up | Stable counter/table | Advance only after 2 sessions hitting 15 reps × all rounds |
| Hard | Knee push-up | None | Floor-based, hips stay in line with knees |
| Advanced | Full push-up | None | Week 13+ baseline target |

## Exercise 02: Squat Progression (starts: Assisted Squat)
> Difficulty: ★☆☆ | Target: Quadriceps | Equipment: Sturdy chair/counter for support (early rungs)
### Target Muscles
- **Prime Mover**: Quadriceps (front thigh muscle)
- **Synergist**: Gluteus maximus (buttock muscle), Hamstrings (back thigh), Adductors (inner thigh)
- **Stabilizer**: Erector spinae (lower back muscles), Rectus abdominis (core)
### How to Perform
1. **Starting Position**: Feet shoulder-width or slightly wider, toes turned out slightly. For the assisted rung, lightly hold a stable counter/door frame for balance only, not to pull body weight up.
2. **Execution (Concentric)**: Drive through the whole foot (weight in heels/mid-foot) to stand back up, knees tracking over the toes — exhale.
3. **Peak Contraction**: Brief pause standing tall, glutes squeezed, 1 second.
4. **Return (Eccentric)**: Push hips back and bend knees to sit down into the squat, comfortable depth (femur roughly parallel, not forced deeper) — inhale.
5. **Repetition**: Reset a neutral spine at the top before each rep — don't chain reps with a rounding back.
### Breathing
- On the way up (standing): exhale
- On the way down (sitting back): inhale
### Common Mistakes
| Mistake | Risk | Correction Cue |
|---------|------|----------------|
| Knees caving inward (valgus) | Knee joint stress | "Push knees out, track them over your 2nd toe" |
| Chasing full depth too early | Loss of control, low-back rounding, added knee stress at BMI ~30 | "Only go as deep as you can control — depth comes later, not now" |
| Heels lifting off the floor | Balance loss, shifts load to knees | "Weight stays in your heels and mid-foot the whole way" |
| Using the support hand to haul the body up | Defeats the point of the assisted rung | "Fingertip touch only — the legs do the work" |
### Alternative Exercises
| Difficulty | Exercise | Equipment | Notes |
|------------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Easy | Assisted squat | Stable counter/door frame | Hand support for balance only |
| Moderate | Bodyweight squat | None | No hand support once balance is solid |
| Hard | Tempo squat (3s eccentric) | None | Same depth, slow the descent to 3 seconds |
| Advanced | Split squat (rear foot elevated on chair) | Stable chair | ⚠️ Confirm chair is stable and won't slide before loading it |

## Exercise 03: Hinge Progression (starts: Glute Bridge)
> Difficulty: ★☆☆ | Target: Gluteus maximus | Equipment: None (stable chair against wall for Hard rung)
### Target Muscles
- **Prime Mover**: Gluteus maximus (buttock muscle)
- **Synergist**: Hamstrings (back thigh muscle)
- **Stabilizer**: Erector spinae (lower back), Rectus abdominis (core)
### How to Perform
1. **Starting Position**: Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat on floor hip-width apart, arms flat by sides.
2. **Execution (Concentric)**: Drive through heels and lift hips off the floor — exhale.
3. **Peak Contraction**: Squeeze glutes hard at the top, body in a straight line from shoulders to knees, hold briefly.
4. **Return (Eccentric)**: Lower hips back down under control, don't let them drop — inhale.
5. **Repetition**: Keep ribs down (don't overarch the lower back) at the top of every rep.
### Breathing
- On the way up (hips lift): exhale
- On the way down (hips lower): inhale
### Common Mistakes
| Mistake | Risk | Correction Cue |
|---------|------|----------------|
| Overarching lower back at the top | Lumbar strain instead of glute work | "Squeeze glutes, not lower back — ribs stay down" |
| Feet too far from hips | Reduces glute activation, adds hamstring cramp risk | "Heels roughly under your knees when bent" |
| Rushing the eccentric (hips drop) | Loses tension, wastes the rep | "Lower for 2 full seconds, don't just let go" |
### Alternative Exercises
| Difficulty | Exercise | Equipment | Notes |
|------------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Easy | Glute bridge | None | Start here (Week 1) |
| Moderate | Single-leg glute bridge | None | One foot planted, other leg extended straight |
| Hard | Hip thrust (shoulders/feet elevated on chair) | Stable chair, against a wall | ⚠️ Fragile-home flag: chair MUST be braced against a wall before use — check it doesn't slide under load before the first rep |

## Exercise 04: Pull Progression (starts: Reverse Snow Angel)
> Difficulty: ★☆☆ | Target: Trapezius (mid/lower) | Equipment: None (resistance band optional from Moderate rung)
### Target Muscles
- **Prime Mover**: Middle/lower trapezius and rear deltoid (upper-back and rear-shoulder muscles)
- **Synergist**: Rhomboids (between shoulder blades), Infraspinatus/teres minor (rotator cuff, external rotators)
- **Stabilizer**: Erector spinae (lower back), core (keeps hips pinned to floor)
### How to Perform
1. **Starting Position**: Lie face-down, arms extended overhead in a "Y," forehead resting or hovering just off the floor, eyes toward the floor.
2. **Execution (Concentric)**: Sweep arms out and down to sides (like a snow angel) while lifting arms and upper chest slightly off the floor — exhale.
3. **Peak Contraction**: Squeeze shoulder blades together at the "T"/"W" position, brief hold.
4. **Return (Eccentric)**: Reverse the sweep back to the overhead "Y," lowering under control — inhale.
5. **Repetition**: Keep the neck neutral throughout — the head does not lead the movement.
### Breathing
- On the way up (arms lift/sweep): exhale
- On the way down (arms lower): inhale
### Common Mistakes
| Mistake | Risk | Correction Cue |
|---------|------|----------------|
| Craning neck up to look forward | Cervical strain | "Eyes stay down at the floor the whole time" |
| Yanking the band instead of controlled sweep | Reduced muscle activation, momentum injury risk | "Slow and controlled — squeeze the shoulder blades, don't jerk" |
| Band anchored to a door handle or light furniture | ⚠️ Anchor failure — object can snap toward face/body | "Anchor only to a fixed, load-rated point (heavy furniture leg bolted to wall, purpose-built anchor) — never a door handle or hinge side" |
### Alternative Exercises
| Difficulty | Exercise | Equipment | Notes |
|------------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Easy | Reverse snow angel | None | Fully sufficient on its own if no band — do not treat as optional filler |
| Moderate | Band row | Resistance band, safe fixed anchor | ⚠️ See anchor-safety note above before using |
| Hard | Band row w/ pause | Resistance band, safe fixed anchor | Add 1–2s pause at peak contraction |

## Exercise 05: Back Extension Progression (starts: Superman Hold)
> Difficulty: ★☆☆ | Target: Erector spinae | Equipment: None
### Target Muscles
- **Prime Mover**: Erector spinae (lower back muscles)
- **Synergist**: Gluteus maximus (buttock), Hamstrings (back thigh)
- **Stabilizer**: Rear deltoids (rear shoulder), core (anti-rotation)
### How to Perform
1. **Starting Position**: Lie face-down, arms extended overhead, legs extended, eyes toward the floor.
2. **Execution (Concentric)**: Simultaneously lift arms, chest, and legs a few inches off the floor — exhale.
3. **Peak Contraction**: Hold the lifted position (this is an isometric hold exercise), 15–20s per the Week-1 prescription.
4. **Return (Eccentric)**: Lower back down under control at the end of the hold — inhale.
5. **Repetition**: Keep the neck neutral (eyes down) for the entire hold — do not tip the head back to "lift higher."
### Breathing
- Into the hold (lifting): exhale
- Out of the hold (lowering): inhale; breathe normally/shallow during the hold itself
### Common Mistakes
| Mistake | Risk | Correction Cue |
|---------|------|----------------|
| Looking up / hyperextending the neck | Cervical strain | "Eyes stay on the floor the entire hold" |
| Lifting excessively high | Lumbar hyperextension | "A few inches is enough — this isn't about height, it's about the squeeze" |
| Holding the breath the whole time | Unnecessary tension, dizziness | "Keep breathing lightly during the hold, don't lock your breath" |
### Alternative Exercises
| Difficulty | Exercise | Equipment | Notes |
|------------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Easy | Superman hold | None | Start here (Week 1) |
| Moderate | Superman alternating arm/leg raise | None | Opposite arm/leg lifts, alternating |
| Hard | Superman pulses | None | Small controlled pulses at the top of the hold |

## Exercise 06: Core Progression (starts: Dead Bug)
> Difficulty: ★☆☆ | Target: Deep abdominals (transverse abdominis) | Equipment: None
### Target Muscles
- **Prime Mover**: Transverse abdominis / Rectus abdominis (deep core / "six-pack" muscle)
- **Synergist**: Obliques (side core), Hip flexors (front hip)
- **Stabilizer**: Erector spinae (lower back — its job here is to NOT move)
### How to Perform
1. **Starting Position**: Lie on back, arms reaching straight up toward ceiling, knees bent 90° over hips (tabletop position). Press lower back flat into the floor.
2. **Execution (Concentric — the "return to center" is actually the effortful phase)**: Slowly extend one arm overhead and the opposite leg straight out, just above the floor — exhale as the limbs extend out.
3. **Peak Contraction**: Brief pause at full extension, lower back still pinned flat to the floor.
4. **Return (Eccentric)**: Draw the arm and leg back to the starting tabletop position under control — inhale.
5. **Repetition**: Alternate sides. If the lower back lifts off the floor at any point, the range of motion is too big — shrink it.
### Breathing
- On the way out (limbs extend): exhale
- On the way back (limbs return): inhale
### Common Mistakes
| Mistake | Risk | Correction Cue |
|---------|------|----------------|
| Lower back arches off the floor | Lumbar strain, defeats the exercise's purpose | "Keep your lower back glued to the floor — if it lifts, the rep is too big" |
| Moving both limbs too fast | Loses core tension, momentum instead of control | "Slow enough that you could stop and hold at any point" |
| Holding breath / bracing too hard | Unnecessary tension, dizziness | "Breathe out as the arm and leg extend" |
### Alternative Exercises
| Difficulty | Exercise | Equipment | Notes |
|------------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Easy | Dead bug | None | Start here (Week 1) |
| Moderate | Slow-tempo dead bug (4s) | None | Same movement, 4-second extend and 4-second return |
| Hard | Hollow body hold (short holds) | None | Beginner-scaled — knees bent option ok, keep holds short and stop if back arches |

## Exercise 07: Isometric Leg Progression (starts: Wall Sit)
> Difficulty: ★☆☆ | Target: Quadriceps | Equipment: Wall
### Target Muscles
- **Prime Mover**: Quadriceps (front thigh muscle)
- **Synergist**: Gluteus maximus (buttock), Hamstrings (back thigh)
- **Stabilizer**: Core (rectus abdominis/obliques), Calves (gastrocnemius)
### How to Perform
1. **Starting Position**: Back flat against a wall, feet out in front, slide down until knees are bent to roughly 90°, as if sitting in an invisible chair.
2. **Execution (Concentric)**: There's no lifting phase — settle into the hold position by pressing the back flat into the wall and weight into the heels — exhale as you settle in.
3. **Peak Contraction**: Hold the position for the prescribed time (15–20s Week 1), knees tracking over toes, weight in heels.
4. **Return (Eccentric)**: Slide back up the wall to standing at the end of the hold — inhale.
5. **Repetition**: Rest fully between rounds before the next hold — this is a high-fatigue isometric, don't rush the next set.
### Breathing
- Settling into the hold: exhale
- During the hold: steady, continuous breathing — never hold your breath
- Standing back up: inhale
### Common Mistakes
| Mistake | Risk | Correction Cue |
|---------|------|----------------|
| Knees past 90° (too deep) or knees caving in | Increased patellar (kneecap) stress | "Aim for a right angle at the knee, knees pointing straight ahead" |
| Holding the breath during the hold | Blood pressure spike, dizziness | "Keep breathing normally the whole hold, don't clench" |
| Weight shifted onto toes | Overloads the knee joint | "Push weight back into your heels" |

⚠️ Stop the hold immediately if sharp or pinching knee pain appears — general muscular burn/fatigue is expected and fine, joint pain is not.
### Alternative Exercises
| Difficulty | Exercise | Equipment | Notes |
|------------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Easy | Wall sit | Wall | Start here (Week 1) |
| Moderate | Wall sit w/ heel raise | Wall | Add a slow heel raise/lower during the hold |
| Hard | Single-leg-assisted wall sit | Wall | Shift most weight to one leg, other leg lightly assisting |

## Cool-Down Routine (5–10 min)
| # | Stretch | Time | Target Area |
|---|---------|------|--------------|
| 1 | Doorframe/wall chest stretch | 20–30s each side | Pectoralis major (chest) — undoes push-up tightness |
| 2 | Child's pose | 30s | Erector spinae (lower back), lats |
| 3 | Cross-body shoulder stretch | 20–30s each side | Rear deltoid, trapezius — undoes pull-pattern work |
| 4 | Standing quad stretch (hold wall for balance) | 20–30s each side | Quadriceps — undoes squat/wall sit load |
| 5 | Seated forward fold | 30s | Hamstrings, lower back |
| 6 | Glute/figure-4 stretch (lying down) | 20–30s each side | Gluteus maximus — undoes bridge/hip thrust work |
| 7 | Hip flexor stretch (half-kneeling) | 20–30s each side | Hip flexors — undoes dead bug/Superman work |

All stretches: hold within a pain-free range, no bouncing, breathe steadily throughout.
